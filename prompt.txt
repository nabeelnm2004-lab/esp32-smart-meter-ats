You are a senior embedded systems engineer specializing in IoT energy management devices.

Your task is to generate complete production-level firmware for an ESP8266-based Smart Automatic Energy Meter Switching System.

The firmware must be robust, modular, and safe for controlling AC power relays.

---

## PROJECT OVERVIEW

This device automatically switches electrical load between three electricity meters based on energy consumption limits.

The goal is to distribute electricity usage across multiple meters automatically.

Example use cases:

* Hostels
* Apartment buildings
* Shops with multiple electricity meters

---

## HARDWARE COMPONENTS

Controller:
ESP8266 NodeMCU

Energy Measurement:
PZEM-004T V3 energy meter module

Switching:
3 relay modules controlling three meter inputs

Timekeeping:
DS3231 RTC module for accurate time and monthly reset

Inputs:
3 push buttons

* Manual Select
* Manual Bypass
* Emergency OFF

Outputs:

* Buzzer
* 3 relays

Load:
Single AC load

---

## POWER ARCHITECTURE

Meter1 → Relay1
Meter2 → Relay2
Meter3 → Relay3

Relay outputs combine to a single line.

Combined output → CT sensor → Load.

Neutral wires from all meters are common.

Only one relay must be active at any time.

---

## ENERGY MEASUREMENT LOGIC

Only ONE PZEM module is used.

The PZEM is placed after the relay outputs so it automatically measures the active meter.

Measurements required:

Voltage
Current
Power
Energy (kWh)

---

## AUTOMATIC SWITCHING LOGIC

Each meter has a user-defined energy limit.

Example:

Meter1 limit = 5 units
Meter2 limit = 5 units
Meter3 limit = 5 units

When energy consumed reaches the limit for the active meter:

1. Turn OFF current relay
2. Wait 500 milliseconds
3. Turn ON next relay

Switch order:

Meter1 → Meter2 → Meter3

If a meter is disabled, it must be skipped.

---

## MONTHLY RESET SYSTEM

Using DS3231 RTC.

At:

Day = 1
Time = 00:00

System must:

Reset energy counters
Reset meter sequence
Start again from Meter1

## BUTTON BEHAVIOR

Manual Select:
Cycles through meters manually.

Manual Bypass:
Disables automatic switching and locks the selected relay.

Emergency OFF:
Immediately turns OFF all relays and triggers buzzer.

---

## SAFETY REQUIREMENTS

Two relays must never be ON simultaneously.

Safe switching sequence must always be:

RelayX OFF
delay 500 ms
RelayY ON
